FAQ - Habit Flow

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Habit Flow?

Habit Flow is a habit tracker that helps users build positive daily habits and stay consistent.

How do I add a habit?

Log in to your dashboard, enter the habit name, and click the Add Habit button.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your habit data is saved in your browser using localStorage.

Can I delete a habit?

Yes. You can delete or update your habits anytime from the dashboard.

Is Habit Flow easy to use?

Yes, Mark your habits as completed each day to view your progress and maintain consistency.

Who can use Habit Flow?

Habit Flow helps you stay organized, build consistency, and achieve your personal goals.

Why should I use Habit Flow?

Anyone who wants to build positive habits, improve productivity, and achieve personal goals can use Habit Flow.

Can I mark a habit as completed?

Yes,You can mark your habits as completed after finishing them for the day.
